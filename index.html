About

Paul Lee

Paul Lee is the founder of Prescient Road, where he works with family offices and venture investors on manager selection, portfolio construction, and direct investment opportunities.

Over more than two decades investing in and building technology companies, Paul co-founded BuildersVC, where he serves as Managing Partner, and founded Roniin LLC, where he served as Founder and CEO. He was previously a General Partner at Lightbank, where he led early-stage investments including Udemy, and a founding partner of Peacock Equity Fund, a venture investment platform formed by NBC Universal and GE Capital. The through line: not only investing in companies, but building investment firms and companies from the ground up.

Today, Paul works with family offices in the US and internationally that are entering venture capital as an asset class. Select work with emerging managers keeps him close to how the next generation of venture firms is being built, and to what LPs will be asked to evaluate.
